WHAT IS EBURG?

EBURG is the best place to Imagine with Friends™. With the largest user-generated online gaming platform, and over 15 million games created by users, EBURG is the #1 gaming site for kids and teens (comScore). Every day, virtual explorers come to EBURG to create adventures, play games, role play, and learn with their friends in a family-friendly, immersive, 3D environment.

EBURG ON YOUR DEVICE

You can access EBURG on PC, Mac, iOS, Android, Amazon Devices, and Xbox One. EBURG adventures are accessible from any device, so players can imagine with their friends regardless of where they are.
